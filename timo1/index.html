About Us

At HI-RES Digital Mapping UG LTD, we combine precision drone technology with expert insights to deliver high-resolution aerial solutions. Our services cover everything from topographic mapping and 3D modeling to detailed inspections and UAV-based surveys.

We believe in clarity from above — providing clients with the data they need to plan, build, and grow smarter. Our certified pilots and GIS professionals are ready to elevate your project with safety, innovation, and accuracy at the core of everything we do.
